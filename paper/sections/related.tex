\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Harmful fine-tuning.} Fine-tuning an aligned model on a handful of harmful examples, or even on benign
data, erodes its safety \citep{qi2024finetuning,yang2023shadow,lermen2023lora,zhan2024removing}; see
\citet{huang2024survey} for a survey of attacks and defenses. Defenses constrain the fine-tune, e.g.\ Lisa's proximal
alternating updates \citep{huang2024lisa} or gradient surgery against an alignment gradient \citep{yi2025safegrad},
or make the weights themselves harder to tune toward harm \citep{henderson2023selfdestructing,rosati2024representation,
tamirisa2025tamper}. We test released defenses against an attack budget a hobbyist has (one epoch of QLoRA on a free
GPU), not against the adaptive attacker these papers often assume.

\paragraph{Refusal directions and abliteration.} Refusal in chat models is mediated by a single residual-stream
direction that can be projected out of the weights \citep{arditi2024refusal}; safety-critical weights are sparse
and easy to remove \citep{wei2024brittleness}, and alignment is often only a few tokens deep \citep{qi2025shallow}.
Our A3 follows \citet{arditi2024refusal}'s recipe, including their causal layer selection and KL filter.

\paragraph{Compression and safety.} Quantization can change a model's trustworthiness
\citep{hong2024decoding} and can even be exploited to hide malicious behavior that appears only after quantizing
\citep{egashira2024exploiting}. We ask the converse: does a common 3-bit scheme erase a fix?

\paragraph{Cheap and cross-lingual jailbreaks.} Best-of-N sampling of random augmentations jailbreaks strong models
\citep{hughes2024bon}. Low-resource languages bypass safety training \citep{yong2023lowresource,deng2024multilingual};
our multilingual slice asks whether that gap interacts with post-release changes.

\paragraph{Judges.} Safety evaluations rest on automatic classifiers such as Llama Guard \citep{inan2023llamaguard}
and WildGuard \citep{han2024wildguard}, or on LLM-as-judge rubrics \citep{zheng2023judging,souly2024strongreject}.
Benchmarks fix prompts and scoring \citep{mazeika2024harmbench,chao2024jailbreakbench}, but judge reliability under
distribution shift, such as garbled or template-free text, is rarely measured against humans. We do so.
